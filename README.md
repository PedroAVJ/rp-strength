# RP Strength

RP Strength is the user's gym and hypertrophy-training program. It gives
gym work a stable plugin owner while keeping the actual training record in the
user-selected private Google Sheet.

The plugin currently provides routing and operating rules only. It does not
claim access to the commercial RP Hypertrophy app or expose an RP service API.
When Google Drive access is available, the installed Google Drive plugin is the
transport for reading or updating the Sheet.

Personal workout history stays in Google Drive and must not be committed to
this repository.
